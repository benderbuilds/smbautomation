## Issues Not Fixed — Requiring Manual Attention

### P1 — No issues requiring urgent manual intervention found.

### P2

- **`app/(site)/blog/page.tsx` title tag** — The blog index uses `title: 'Business Automation Blog'` with no `seoTitle` override. The page's H1 reads "Automation insights for business owners." Consider a keyword-richer title like `"Small Business Automation Blog | SMB Automation"` to capture branded + category queries.

- **`app/(site)/blog/page.tsx` missing JSON-LD** — The `/blog` index page has no structured data. A `Blog` or `CollectionPage` schema would give AI engines a stronger signal about the nature of the page. Requires code change to `app/(site)/blog/page.tsx`.

### P3

- **hrt-patient-reactivation-automation.mdx and trt-* posts** — These posts are topically narrow (HRT/TRT/GLP-1) and likely receive low organic traffic relative to the core service-business posts. No structural issues, but SEO ROI from additional optimization effort here is lower than for higher-volume verticals.

- **og:image** — No custom `og:image` is set on posts. The site uses a generated opengraph image (`opengraph-image.tsx`). A static branded OG image per post would improve social share CTR. P3 given complexity.

- **Breadcrumb schema** — The blog post layout has no `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD. Adding it would strengthen SERP appearance with breadcrumb rich results. Low lift but not critical.

## Recommendations for Next Sprint

1. **Add Blog schema to `/blog` index** — A single JSON-LD block in `app/(site)/blog/page.tsx` with `@type: Blog`, listing the 5 most recent posts as `blogPost` items, signals content structure to AI engines.

2. **Track keyword rankings on top 10 posts** — Use Google Search Console or a rank-tracker to monitor Map Pack and organic positions for high-traffic posts (hvac-automation-seasonal-surge, plumbing-business-automation-playbook, auto-repair-shop-automation-playbook). Set up monthly reporting against baseline.

3. **Canonical audit** — Confirm that `smbautomation.io` (no www) and `www.smbautomation.io` both resolve to canonical URLs. All canonicals in code point to non-www; server-side redirect must match.

4. **Review velocity for GEO** — Publish 2–3 more posts linking to the 3 newly-fixed posts (foundation-repair, gym, quote-form) to eliminate any residual thin-linking risk as the site grows.

5. **Structured FAQ schema** — The FAQ sections in posts output as raw MDX. Adding `FAQPage` JSON-LD alongside the `BlogPosting` schema would surface FAQ rich results in Google and make Q&A content more citable by AI assistants. This is the highest-GEO-impact technical item for next sprint.
