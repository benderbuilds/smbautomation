## Recommendations for Next Sprint

### P1 (High Priority)

1. **Orphan check with reverse-link index** — verify that all 85 posts are linked from at least one other post (not just that each post links outward). The current audit checks outbound links but not inbound. A full reverse-link audit would catch any posts not being discovered by internal crawlers.

2. **seoTitle freshness** — several older posts (2026-03, 2026-04) use seoTitles that predate the current keyword strategy. Review the healthcare and vertical-specific posts for alignment with current ranking targets.

3. **Upgrade `target: "es5"` in tsconfig.json** — the `target: "es5"` compiler option is deprecated. Upgrading to `"es2017"` or `"es2020"` would allow removing `ignoreDeprecations` entirely and reduces the transpilation overhead Next.js carries.

### P2 (Medium Priority)

4. **Open Graph images** — the `/blog/[slug]/opengraph-image` route exists but there is no per-post image generation. Adding dynamic OG images (post title + brand) improves click-through rate on social sharing and AI-cited previews.

5. **dateModified in BlogPosting schema** — the JSON-LD only includes `datePublished`. Adding `dateModified` (updated when a post is edited) signals freshness to Google and AI crawlers that factor recency into citations.

6. **FAQ structured data** — all posts have FAQ sections in prose, but none output `FAQPage` JSON-LD. Adding this schema makes the Q&A directly eligible for Google's FAQ rich results and improves AI assistant citation confidence for question-answer queries.

### P3 (Lower Priority)

7. **Breadcrumb schema** — add `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD on post pages for richer SERP presentation.

8. **Article word count signal** — GEO crawlers (Perplexity, Claude) weight longer, denser content more heavily. Consider auditing the shortest posts (<1500 words) for expansion opportunities.

9. **Internal link anchor text audit** — some internal links use generic anchors like "learn more" or "book a free consult." Keyword-rich anchor text improves both PageRank distribution and AI citation relevance.
