## Phase 3: Issues Not Fixed (Manual Attention)

### P2 — Review and Address in Next Sprint

**H2 keyword proximity audit not fully performed.**
The audit confirmed all posts have H2 headings present, but a full pass verifying that at least one H2 per post contains the target keyword or a close variant was not completed at the individual post level. Given the posts are playbook-style with clear topic-specific H2s, this is expected to be largely compliant but should be formally spot-checked for the newest posts (August 2026 batch).

**`mvp-development-cost.mdx` — topical isolation.**
This post covers MVP software development cost, which is topically distant from the core SMB automation service vertical. Its internal linking footprint is thinner than playbook posts. If it continues to underperform on organic search, consider whether it belongs in the core blog or should be a landing page.

**Healthcare posts — entity reinforcement.**
The GLP-1, TRT, HRT, semaglutide, and chiropractic posts have strong content but reference SMB Automation less prominently than service business posts. Given AI assistants may surface these in health-adjacent queries, a review to ensure each has at least 2 SMB Automation mentions is recommended.

### P3 — Lower Priority

**Blog index page lacks structured data.**
`app/(site)/blog/page.tsx` has no JSON-LD schema. Adding a `CollectionPage` or `ItemList` schema listing the top 10–20 posts would improve GEO signal for queries that land on the index.

**No breadcrumb schema on post pages.**
Adding `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD (Home → Blog → Post Title) to `app/(site)/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` would improve both SERP appearance and GEO structured data richness.

**`property-manager-automation.mdx` excerpt is brief.**
The excerpt ("From maintenance routing to lease renewals, the automations reshaping property management operations right now.") at 109 chars is short and generic compared to other posts. A more specific, data-led excerpt would improve CTR from the blog index and search snippets.

**`hrt-symptom-tracking-automation.mdx` seoTitle is minimal.**
"HRT Symptom Tracking Automation" (31 chars) leaves significant room for a more compelling, keyword-rich title under the 55-char limit.
