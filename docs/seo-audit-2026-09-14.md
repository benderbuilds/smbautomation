## Issues Not Fixed — Manual Attention Required

### P2 — `ctaType` frontmatter missing from 62 posts

62 of 83 blog posts do not have a `ctaType` frontmatter field. The code in `lib/posts.ts` defaults to `'broad'` when missing, so this is not a production error. However, explicit `ctaType` would make CTA strategy intentional and auditable.

**Recommendation:** For next sprint, add `ctaType: "workflow"` or `ctaType: "broad"` to all posts based on content type. Workflow posts (lead follow-up, specific automation systems) should use `"workflow"`; strategy and playbook posts should use `"broad"`.

### P3 — No `image` / `og:image` in post frontmatter

Open Graph posts lack post-specific `og:image` tags beyond the auto-generated opengraph-image.tsx. Custom OG images per post would improve social sharing click-through rates and GEO citation richness.

**Recommendation:** Consider adding an `image` frontmatter field and referencing it in the `generateMetadata` function for post-specific OG image URLs.

### P3 — No `dateModified` in BlogPosting schema

The `BlogPosting` JSON-LD outputs `datePublished` but no `dateModified`. Google and AI assistants use `dateModified` to assess content freshness, which is a significant GEO ranking signal.

**Recommendation:** Add a `lastModified` frontmatter field to posts and include it as `dateModified` in the JSON-LD output in `app/(site)/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`.

## Recommendations for Next Sprint

1. **Add `dateModified` to JSON-LD** — Update `app/(site)/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` to output `dateModified` alongside `datePublished`. Add `lastModified` frontmatter to posts when content is updated.

2. **Add `ctaType` to all posts explicitly** — Remove reliance on the default fallback; make CTA strategy visible in frontmatter.

3. **Add `author` entity to BlogPosting schema** — Expand the `author` object in JSON-LD to include `url: 'https://smbautomation.io'` and optionally a `Person` entity for E-E-A-T signals.

4. **Implement post-specific OG images** — Using `next/og` and the existing `opengraph-image.tsx` pattern, generate post-specific social images that include the post title and a consistent brand treatment.

5. **Add `WebPage` or `FAQPage` schema** — For posts that contain FAQ sections, consider supplementing `BlogPosting` with `FAQPage` schema to target featured snippet and "People also ask" placements.

6. **Evaluate adding more AI bot rules to robots.ts** — Consider explicit `allow` rules for `Anthropic-AI`, `YouBot`, and `BingBot` to ensure complete AI crawler coverage.
