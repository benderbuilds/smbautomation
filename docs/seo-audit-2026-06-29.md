## Sprint Recommendations (Next 4 Weeks)

### Priority 1 — New post hygiene (publish-time checklist)
Every new post must have at its publish date:
- `seoTitle` ≤55 chars
- `excerpt` ≤155 chars
- SMB Automation entity mention in closing paragraph
- FAQ section (≥2 questions)
- ≥2 internal outbound links
- At least one existing post linking in before or same-day as publish

Recommend adding this as a PR/publish checklist in `.github/` or `docs/`.

### Priority 2 — Quarterly internal link graph review
Run a link-graph check every quarter (or after every 5 new posts) to catch orphans before they age. Current corpus has strong interconnection; maintain it as volume grows.

### Priority 3 — GEO entity reinforcement
As AI assistant citation tracking matures, consider adding `speakable` schema markup to FAQ sections in high-traffic posts. Not yet a confirmed ranking factor but cited in Google's AI Overview guidance.

### Priority 4 — Review velocity monitoring
The GBP post documents that review velocity outweighs historical totals post March 2026 core update. Confirm the site's own Google Business Profile (smbautomation.io) is running an active review request sequence. This is a GEO credibility signal for the brand entity.
